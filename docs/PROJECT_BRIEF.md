# Pocket Quests — Project Brief

## Vision

Pocket Quests is a cute, game-inspired web app that makes everyday activities, hobbies, and personal goals more fun and rewarding.

It encourages users to spend their free time intentionally, make small, regular efforts, and enjoy their progress instead of defaulting to scrolling. The goal is not maximum productivity, but a more playful and enjoyable daily life.

## Target Users

People who have hobbies, interests, or personal goals and want a more playful way to build routines and make consistent progress.

## Core Experience

Pocket Quests is a personal quest journal built around reusable activities. Users create or discover quests, complete them with one tap, and see their progress accumulate over time.

The experience should feel like a light, cozy game rather than another productivity tool or chore.

## MVP Scope

- Create, view, edit, and delete reusable quests.
- Complete quests with one tap.
- Store quests and completion history locally in the browser.
- Display basic progress for each quest.
- Use a responsive, mobile-first design that works on phones and desktops.
- Provide simple, satisfying visual feedback when a quest is completed.

Optional onboarding with hobby categories and predefined quest suggestions may be included if time allows.

## Future Possibilities

- Category-based progress and streaks.
- Collectible achievement badges.
- Quest cards that evolve as progress builds.
- Personal challenges and a "Surprise me" feature.
- A plant, animal, or other companion that grows with the user's progress.
- Accounts and cloud synchronisation across devices.

## Product Principles

- **Progress over productivity pressure:** Small efforts count.
- **Simplicity first:** Completing a quest should be effortless.
- **Playfulness:** The app should feel cute, cozy, and rewarding.
- **User ownership:** Users choose their activities and goals.
- **Incremental development:** Build a useful core experience before introducing complex game mechanics.

## Technical Direction

- React web application.
- Responsive, mobile-first interface.
- Browser local storage for the initial version.
- No backend or user accounts in the MVP.

The architecture should remain simple while allowing future expansion.
